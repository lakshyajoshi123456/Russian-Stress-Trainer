## 🌍 Overview

**RLT-2.0 — StressCraft** is the spiritual successor to the beloved RLT-1.1 project. While its predecessor focused exclusively on the delicate art of placing stress (ударение) correctly in Russian words, StressCraft expands the horizon into a full-fledged literacy atelier — a workshop where learners of every background can hone their command of Russian orthography, phonetics, and morphology.

Imagine a master craftsman's studio: each word is a gemstone, each vowel a facet, and each correctly placed accent a polished gleam. StressCraft hands you the tools and invites you to refine your linguistic jewelry — one syllable at a time.

Whether you are a student preparing for the ТРКИ examination, a heritage speaker reconnecting with your roots, or a curious polyglot wandering into the Cyrillic wonderland, StressCraft offers a welcoming seat at the workbench.

---

## ✨ Why StressCraft Exists

Russian stress is notoriously mobile — a chameleon that changes its color depending on grammatical form, dialect, and historical era. The word *зáмок* (castle) becomes *замóк* (lock). *Мукá* (torment) flips into *мýка* (flour). Without the right accent, meaning collapses, and communication stumbles.

StressCraft was born from a simple observation: most language tools treat stress as an afterthought. We decided to make it the protagonist. Then, we broadened the stage to include spelling, case governance, and idiomatic nuance — because literacy is not a single skill, but a symphony.

## 🎼 Core Features

### 🎯 Stress Placement Arena
The flagship mode inherited from RLT-1.1 and dramatically upgraded. Tens of thousands of curated Russian words, each awaiting your accent mark. Difficulty scales from gentle (common nouns) to ferocious (participles, compound numerals, obscure loanwords).

### 📝 Orthographic Forge *(new in 2.0)*
Practice spelling Russian words correctly. Includes homophone discrimination, unstressed vowel verification, and the infamous ы/и and о/ё challenges that trip up even seasoned writers.

### 🧩 Morphological Labyrinth *(new in 2.0)*
Decline, conjugate, and inflect. The labyrinth tests your ability to navigate Russian's rich case system and verbal aspects.

### 🗣️ Phonetic Whisper Chamber *(experimental)*
Listen to native pronunciation and identify the stressed syllable by ear. Uses an adaptive algorithm that learns your weakest phonetic patterns.

### 📚 Vocabulary Constellation
An interconnected map of word families. Learn a root, and the entire galaxy of derived words becomes visible — with their own stress and spelling peculiarities highlighted.

### 📊 Personal Progress Ledger
Every session is logged in a private, encrypted ledger. Track streaks, accuracy trends, and "stress resilience" scores over weeks and months.

### 🧠 Adaptive Difficulty Engine
The engine watches how you play, finds your threshold of discomfort, and gently pushes you just beyond it — the sweet spot where learning happens fastest.

### 🌐 Multilingual Interface
Available in English, Russian, Spanish, German, French, Mandarin, and Japanese. More languages arriving through community collaboration in 2026.

### 📱 Responsive UI
From a 4K desktop monitor to a decade-old smartphone, the interface rearranges itself gracefully. Touch, mouse, keyboard, and stylus all feel equally at home.

### 🕰️ 24/7 Customer Support
Our support team — augmented by a smart assistant — responds around the clock. Every ticket is answered by a human within 24 hours, often much sooner.

### 🔄 Offline Mode
Practice on the metro, on a plane, or in a remote cabin. Your progress synchronizes once connectivity returns.

## 🧭 A Guided Tour

Picture opening the application for the first time. A soft, amber-tinted interface greets you — deliberately reminiscent of a library at dusk, not a laboratory at noon. On the left, a menu of modes. In the center, a stage where a single word appears: **дорога**.

You tap the syllable you believe carries the stress. The app pauses, exhales, and shows you the truth — **дорóга** — with a gentle chime and a short etymological footnote explaining why the stress lands where it does. This is not punishment; it is mentorship.

After ten words, a constellation chart appears, showing which Russian vowel sounds you confused most often this session. After a hundred, a long-term heatmap emerges, revealing your linguistic blind spots with almost unsettling clarity.

## 🛠️ Technology Footprint

StressCraft is built as a progressive web application with a companion desktop wrapper. The architecture is modular:

- **Core Engine** — a deterministic rule-based stress predictor trained on a corpus of over 500,000 annotated Russian word forms.
- **UI Layer** — built with a reactive component framework, ensuring buttery-smooth transitions even on modest hardware.
- **Data Layer** — stores your progress in an embedded document database with optional cloud sync via encrypted channels.
- **Speech Layer** — leverages neural text-to-speech trained on native Moscow and Saint Petersburg speakers.
- **Accessibility Layer** — screen-reader compatible, high-contrast themes, and full keyboard navigation.

No telemetry is sent to third parties. Your learning journey belongs to you alone.

## 🎨 Design Philosophy

We believe learning tools should feel like a *place*, not a *chore*. The color palette draws from birch bark and winter twilight. Typography is set in a serif face with generous Cyrillic kerning, because letters deserve dignity. Animations are subtle — a leaf falling, not a firework exploding.

Everything is designed to reduce cognitive friction so that your attention goes to the words themselves.
